# Domain glossary

[Pump It Up](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pump_It_Up_\(video_game_series\)) (PIU) is a five-panel dance arcade game by [Andamiro](https://www.piugame.com/). Players step on arrows in time with music. This document defines the PIU and project terms the code uses, in a form approachable to people who haven't played the game.

For solution layout and patterns, see [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md). For project conventions, see [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md).

## Game basics

- **Pump It Up** — the arcade series. Current release: **Pump It Up 2024 Phoenix** (May 2024). **Phoenix 2** is announced — its website went live 2026-07-01 at piugame.com (Phoenix's site moved to phoenix.piugame.com), but the game itself has not released yet.
- **Mix** — a version of PIU. Three mixes matter here: **XX** (legacy, pre-Phoenix, its own tables/pages), and **Phoenix** / **Phoenix 2**, which run **in parallel** — both use Phoenix scoring, share `Chart` rows via per-mix `ChartMix` levels, and every score-derived table is keyed by mix. Modeled by `MixEnum`; users switch via the current-mix selector, and several Phoenix 2 surfaces show "Coming soon" until post-launch verification (titles, recommendations, import, official leaderboards).
- **Song** — a piece of music: title, artist, duration, BPM. One song typically has multiple charts. See [`Song`](../ScoreTracker/ScoreTracker.SharedKernel/Models/Song.cs).
- **Chart** — a specific playable arrangement of a song: a difficulty level + chart type (Single, Double, Co-op) + step pattern. A given song often has many charts (e.g. Single S15, Single S20, Double D18, Co-op-2). See [`Chart`](../ScoreTracker/ScoreTracker.SharedKernel/Models/Chart.cs).
- **Difficulty level** — numeric chart rating (e.g. 15, 20, 26). Higher = harder. See [`DifficultyLevel`](../ScoreTracker/ScoreTracker.SharedKernel/ValueTypes/DifficultyLevel.cs).

## Scoring

- **Phoenix score** — the current scoring scheme. 0–1,000,000 points, **accuracy-based and not combo-based** — that is the break from XX, which was almost entirely combo-driven. What you are graded on is how you hit the notes. Combo survives only as a 0.5% residual term in the formula: nothing to play around, but enough that **max combo** can be recovered from a score plus its judgement counts. See [`PhoenixScore`](../ScoreTracker/ScoreTracker.SharedKernel/ValueTypes/PhoenixScore.cs) and [`ScoreScreen`](../ScoreTracker/ScoreTracker.Domain/Records/ScoreScreen.cs).
- **XX score** — the pre-Phoenix scoring scheme (combo and letter based). **Legacy** — retained for historical data; new feature work targets Phoenix only.
- **Letter grade** — end-of-chart performance rating. Phoenix and XX have separate letter-grade systems (`PhoenixLetterGrade`, `XXLetterGrade`).
- **Plate** — Phoenix-only secondary rating, based on per-step accuracy distribution. Modeled by `PhoenixPlate`. Awarded only on a **pass**: a broken attempt has no plate anywhere in the system ([score-truth-model.md](design/score-truth-model.md) D8).
- **Personal best** — the attempt in `PhoenixRecord` for a (player, chart, mix). A pass always outranks a break whatever the numbers; below that it is score, with plate as a tiebreak at equal score only. Manual entry (the record forms, the API, a CSV upload) overwrites it and is the only route by which it can go down.
- **Broken** — a **failed, finished** attempt: the life bar reached zero at some point, the song played to its last note, the game graded it (the grade art carries an `x_` prefix), awarded no plate, and every note was judged — so its score is a real chart score. It becomes your record only on a chart you have never passed, and only when you opt in on the import; a later, higher finished fail may replace it, a pass always outranks it. Not to be confused with a stage break ([stage-breaks-and-max-combo.md](design/stage-breaks-and-max-combo.md)).
- **Stage broken** — an **interrupted** attempt: the stage broke and the song ended before its last note. The game assigns no grade; the recently-played pages print `STAGE BREAK` where the score would be, and the Phoenix 2 best list prints the *running* score at the moment of the break (normalised over the notes judged so far — not a chart score, and never stored as one). A stage break is **never a personal best**, from any source, opt-in or not; it is journaled as a play (`IsStageBroken`, no score) so a chart's history and *attempts before this clear* count it. A **walk-off** — a stage break with nothing hit, only misses — is never stored at all ([stage-breaks-and-max-combo.md](design/stage-breaks-and-max-combo.md) D10–D12).
- **Judgment** — the per-step verdict: Perfect, Great, Good, Bad, Miss. Drives both score and the lifebar. Kept in English in every locale. Modeled by `Judgment`; the five counts behind one play are `JudgementCounts`, stored on a record only when the play that set it was observed with them (capture began 2026-07-17, and only a *changed* record is written, so coverage is dense on Phoenix 2 and thin on Phoenix). A judged play whose sum disagrees with the catalog's note count is logged and otherwise left alone — the catalog is write-once and repaired by hand ([stage-breaks-and-max-combo.md](design/stage-breaks-and-max-combo.md) D13).
- **Max combo** — the longest unbroken run of a play. No piugame surface we read carries one, so it is *solved* from the score and the judgement counts by [`PhoenixComboSolver`](../ScoreTracker/ScoreTracker.Domain/Services/PhoenixComboSolver.cs) — and only when those counts cover the whole chart, otherwise the denominator the game scored against is unknown and the answer is null. **Stored** beside the five counts on the record and the journal (`MaxCombo`, solved at write, re-derivable by the *Backfill max combos* admin button), served on API v2 and the CSV export, shown on no page. A Good holds combo without advancing it, so it is never simply `notes − (bads + misses)`. Exact to one combo under ~2,500 notes, one out above.
- **Lifebar** — the health gauge. The **visible bar** is 0–1000 life (full = the rainbow bar); every song starts you at 500. Above 1000 sits the **overflow**, `3 × level²` of life the cabinet never shows you — the only thing a higher level changes. Phoenix 2 runs an electric effect along the bar when the overflow is completely full. A hidden **gain multiplier** (0.1 at song start, capped at 0.8, near-zeroed by a miss and halved by a bad) scales what clean notes pay, which is why misses cost far more than the life they take. Modeled by [`LifebarSimulator`](../ScoreTracker/ScoreTracker.SharedKernel/Models/LifebarSimulator.cs); derived answers live in `LifebarAnalysis` and surface on `/LifeCalculator` ([design](design/life-calculator-redesign.md)). Formulas are an NX2/Prime data-mine, unverified against Phoenix.

## Player progression

- **Pumbility** — a composite player rating computed from a player's top scores; the closest single number to "how good is this player overall." Carried as an **unrounded double everywhere below the UI** and stored as `float`; the presentation layer is the only thing that rounds one, to two decimals for a total and by size for a gain (docs/UX-GUIDELINES.md, ratcheted by `PumbilityPrecisionTests`). Calculated by `PlayerRatingSaga` / `WorldRankingService`, with a **different formula per mix** (`ScoringConfiguration.PumbilityScoring(mix, …)`):
  - **Phoenix**: one mixed top-50 pool; per chart `BaseRating(level) × gradeModifier`, plate-blind.
  - **Phoenix 2** (reverse-engineered from the live site; per-chart values verified to the cent against the official breakdown page `my_page/pumbility.php`, 2026-07-19): **one merged top-50 pool across Singles+Doubles** for the overall value (plus separate per-type top-50 pools behind the Singles/Doubles boards); per chart `Base(pricedLevel) × (gradeMultiplier + plateBonus)` (additive), where `Base = 130 + 5·L + 5·max(0, L−24)` and **singles price one level up the curve** (an S17 is worth `Base(18)`; doubles price at their printed level). **Charts below level 10 price at zero.** **Both constant tables are per chart type**: a Single pays more for an Extreme Game (0.014 vs 0.012) and an Ultimate Game (0.017 vs 0.016) plate, and pays *less* at every grade below AA+ (AA 1.36/1.37, A+ 1.33/1.35, A 1.28/1.30, B 1.20/1.25, C 1.10/1.20, D 1.00/1.10), the gap widening down the ladder until it plateaus at −0.10 across C and D. Those eight cells are the only disagreements; everything else is shared. Never settle one of these constants from a pool of a single chart type — doing exactly that is what put three of them wrong (docs/design/phoenix2-implementation.md). As of 2026-08-14 **every rung of both ladders is a live per-chart reading — F included** (the bottom five cells closed from deliberately-played small-pool charts); the only thing left unmeasured in the formula is `Base(28)`/`Base(29)` (extrapolated — no 28 or 29 has ever been in a pool). **A passing F is a real rung — F 0.90/1.00 — not an exclusion**: the "F prices at zero" ruling was reversed on 2026-08-14 by a deliberately played S12 F priced 176.67 on the breakdown page (the earlier zero having been a sub-10 chart's zero all along), and the Doubles side was measured the same day at 181.44 on a D10 SG. Only CO-OP, UCS, half-double, **broken plays** and **charts below level 10** contribute nothing. **CO-OP is still priced** on Phoenix 2 — a flat base of 100, where Phoenix used a flat 2,000 — but that number surfaces only on the titles page and enters no pool (owner, 2026-08-17); `ScoringConfiguration` keeps its CO-OP base at 2,000 for every mix and zeroes CO-OP in the Phoenix 2 formula through `ChartTypeModifiers`, so the 100 lives beside the calculator page that states it. The site exposes all three values (Total = merged top-50, Singles/Doubles = per-type pools); the title ladder's [S]/[D] tiers gate on the per-type pools and the Total tier on the merged pool. Overall PUMBILITY is NOT Singles + Doubles (corrected 2026-07-13).
- **Levels bought / level-equivalent** — the exchange rate between scoring and passing that `/PumbilityCalculator/{mix}` draws ([design/pumbility-calculator.md](design/pumbility-calculator.md)): a chart's PUMBILITY value expressed as *which level's 900,000 is worth the same*. The baseline is the score 900,000 — exactly the AA floor on Phoenix and exactly the A+ floor on Phoenix 2, so it is one play on both mixes (a grade name would not be) — and `PumbilityLevelEquivalence` in SharedKernel inverts the anchor-grade value curve per chart type to read it. An SSS+ buys about 2.7 levels of pass-pushing at 20 on Phoenix and about 4.6 on Phoenix 2: the grade span shrank 4.5× between the mixes but a level shrank 7.8×, so scoring is the cheaper lever on Phoenix 2 — but only up to **level 23**, where the two curves cross and stay crossed (at 24 an SSS+ buys 3.5 levels on Phoenix against 3.3 Singles / 2.8 Doubles here, because Phoenix 2 prices every level above 24 at double the step).
- **Pumbility level** — Phoenix 2's unnamed sub-steps inside each `[P.B]` gem title: five per gem, splitting the gem's span evenly (200 points across a 1,000-wide gem, 500 across BRONZE/SILVER), plus rung 0 below BRONZE and the ABYSS ABSOLUTE capstone — 37 rungs, badge indices 0–36. The game states a level nowhere in text; the badge image's file index *is* the rung (`Phoenix2PumbilityLevel` in Domain, decoded from the live board — [design/pumbility-levels.md](design/pumbility-levels.md)).
- **PUMBILITY peers** — on Phoenix 2, the players within **±3 rungs** of you on the PUMBILITY level ladder who hold a **full 50-chart pool of the chart type** (you must hold one too, or that type has no peers). One rung — the total pool's — serves both types. It is the *one* definition of "players like me" for PUMBILITY: the `/Pumbility` projection draws its evidence from them (Phoenix 2 scores only, median, hidden under five peers) and the tier lists' PUMBILITY lens counts their pools ([design/pumbility-overhaul.md §4.8](design/pumbility-overhaul.md), [design/pumbility-tier-list.md §5](design/pumbility-tier-list.md)). Phoenix 1 has no level ladder: its projection uses competitive-level bands and its lens the difficulty-title level, both unchanged. The word is **peers** — never "cohort" (owner, 2026-08-15) — and you are never one of your own: every surface that counts or ranks against your peers has you taken out (owner, 2026-08-17).
- **Peers IQR** — beside a projected score on `/Pumbility`, the peers' 25th and 75th percentile scores printed as two letter grades with a dash between them: *AAA+ — AAA+* means the peers agree, *AA+ — SS+* means the chart splits them. Read over exactly the peers and weights the median was (`ScoreProjection.Spreads`), on both mixes. It is the interquartile range and deliberately not the min–max — the extremes are one player each — which is why the label says IQR ([design/pumbility-overhaul.md D30](design/pumbility-overhaul.md)).
- **Competitive Level progress** — a tier/level system tracking a player's competitive standing, driven primarily by Weekly Charts performance. UI at `Pages/Progress/CompetitiveLevel.razor`.
- **Peers** — the settled UI term for *players near your competitive level*: the cohort the bucket-cached machinery (`CohortScoreProvider`, half-level buckets ±0.5) ranks a player's scores against. "You beat 80% of Peers" means your best on that chart beats 80% of that cohort's bests. First shipped in the Hot Streak widget goal; older copy ("competitive matches" on Account Stats) predates the term.

## Community-tracked systems

The features the community uses most:

- **Tier list** — charts categorized into difficulty/skill buckets. Generated by `TierListSaga` from aggregate score data — including the **PUMBILITY tier lists**, which band, per peer group, counts of who holds each chart in their top-50 pool ([design/pumbility-tier-list.md](design/pumbility-tier-list.md)). UI under `Pages/TierLists/`.
- **Badge** — what a chart actually demands, as piucenter measures it: 33 granular tags (`staggered_bracket`, `twist_over90`, `anchor_run`, …) banked per chart with a segment-coverage fraction, plus a top-three dominance pick. This is the site's skill vocabulary. Badges group into five **badge families** — Brackets, Twists, Stamina/Runs, Tech, Doubles Tech — which carry the identity colours (`BadgeCategory`, `PiuCenterBadges`). ⚠ The older `Skill` enum is a lossy 33-into-11 rollup of these, still wired into the tier-list Skill lens and Pumbility and slated for deletion — see [design/nuke-old-skill-categories.md](design/nuke-old-skill-categories.md).
- **Weekly Charts** — a weekly-rotating set of charts the community competes on, scraped from the official PIU site. Refreshed by the recurring `RotateWeeklyChartsCommand`. Drives Competitive Level progress.
- **Community Leaderboards** — per-community (user-formed group: team, friends, region) leaderboards. See [`ICommunityRepository`](../ScoreTracker/ScoreTracker.Communities/Domain/ICommunityRepository.cs).
- **UCS** — User-Created Step: a community-authored chart for a song, ranked separately from official charts. Still a real part of the game; **this site no longer tracks it.** The UCS vertical and its leaderboard page were retired (tables archived, see [DATABASE-SCHEMA.md](DATABASE-SCHEMA.md)). The term survives here because it still appears in scoring rules — UCS plays never count toward PUMBILITY.

## Code-only

- **Census (OfficialMirror only)** — piugame's per-level score-count table (`my_page/play_data.php`) and our locally derived equivalent (`AccountCensus`, `LocalCensusBuilder`, `CensusDiff`): a complete per-level count of an account's recorded scores, diffed against ours to localize missing imports ([design/import-completeness-check.md](design/import-completeness-check.md)). The word is scoped to that one sense — nothing in the tier-list family uses it.
- **Saga (in this codebase)** — a class grouping one MassTransit `IConsumer<>` with related MediatR handlers for a single feature (e.g. `TierListSaga`, `WeeklyTournamentSaga`). **Not** a `MassTransit.MassTransitStateMachine` — those are formal state machines; these are feature-grouped handler classes.
