The head is three things

  • The scope chip — the same chip as the bar's, live: open it and change where this comment goes while you write. It shows a person glyph for Public, a gold lock for Notes, a people glyph for a club; the label is the scope's name, ellipsized past ~120 px.

  • A scope you cannot post in (a mute, the lock) shows in the menu greyed with can't post here; picking it swaps the line for the tab's sentence, as today.
  • The rules card, when owed, renders in place of the line, as today.

What this changes in the code

  • The component renders two pieces: the bar (in flow, under the chips) and the panel (positioned). One island on the page, placed after the chips; the module lays the panel out over the strip and the map from the geometry it already owns, so the panel no longer needs to live inside the viewer.
  • Scope becomes one setting shared by the bar and the composer, kept per host like today; the panel head loses the menu and the ＋.
  • The composer gets a variant without Cancel and the audience sentence, and a slot for the scope chip. The tab's composer is untouched.
  • Panel width = box left to map right, on every host, from the module's layout pass.
  • Empty state shrinks to one line — "Nothing here yet." — since the gesture line lives in the bar now.
  • Three resx keys go (the ＋'s title, the two gesture sentences with "No comments on this chart yet —"), two arrive (the short gesture lines), one is reworded (Note on {0}…). Nine locales, same commit.
