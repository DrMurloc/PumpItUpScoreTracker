# YouTubePlaylists

The Phoenix-era half of the chart-versions backfill ([docs/design/chart-versions.md](../../docs/design/chart-versions.md) §5). Andamiro's channel keeps one playlist per patch per category — `[PIU PHOENIX] v.2.12.0 - STEP CHART VIDEO (Original)`, `[PIU PHOENIX 2] v1.01.0 - STEP CHART VIDEO (K-POP)` — so playlist membership says which patch a chart arrived in. Three scripts, operator-run, deliberately outside `ScoreTracker.sln` like the pumpout extractor next door; their outputs never enter the repo.

## Steps

1. **Inventory the playlists.** Open `youtube.com/@PUMPITUPOfficial/playlists` in a browser and collect `playlistId <tab> declared count <tab> title` for every step-chart playlist (the page shows thirty and loads the rest on scroll; the `lockupViewModel` objects in `ytInitialData` carry `contentId` and the title). `inventory-2026-09-12.tsv` is the list as of that date, every Phoenix content patch from launch through Phoenix 2 1.01.0. Add the new patch's playlists when one ships.
2. **Crawl.** `python crawl-playlists.py inventory.tsv <clientVersion> <apiKey> out` — YouTube's own browse call (`browseId: VL<playlistId>`, then continuations), one JSON per playlist in `out/`. The client version and key come from the page's `ytcfg` (`INNERTUBE_CLIENT_VERSION`, `INNERTUBE_API_KEY`). Four playlists come back one short of their declared count; that is YouTube hiding unavailable videos.
3. **Export the catalog.** Two TSVs from a prod-synced SQL Server, UTF-8 (`sqlcmd -f 65001`, strip the BOM): `chartvideos.tsv` (`VideoUrl, ChartId, Song, Type, OriginalMix, P1Level, P2Level, InXX` from ChartVideo ⋈ Chart ⋈ Song ⋈ Mix) and `phoenixcharts.tsv` (`MixId, Id, Name, Type, Level, OriginalMix, PlayerCount` for every Phoenix and Phoenix 2 ChartMix row).
4. **Join.** `python join.py` — by stored video id first (Phoenix 2.00.0 onward and all of Phoenix 2: our ids are the playlist videos), by title where the id misses (Phoenix 1.00.0–1.08.0: our stored videos are earlier uploads; `[PUMP IT UP PHOENIX] Song (한글) S18, S21`, `CO-OP(x3)`). Writes `playlist-chart-versions.csv` (mix, chartId, song, type, level, origin, version, how) and `report.txt`. The report names the natives no playlist carries — on 2026-09-12 that was 69, and NamuWiki's per-version pages ("Add existing songs") settled every one; append those as rows with `how = wiki`.
5. **Emit.** `python emit-backfill.py playlist-chart-versions.csv [s6-chart-versions-backfill.sql] > chart-versions-backfill.sql` — the assignments, the La Cinquantaine S22/D24 origin fix (D9), the present-at-launch rule for the rest, and the extractor's legacy S6 stitched in, under one `@WhatIf` switch.

## What the 2026-09-12 run found

Phoenix 2: 308 of 308 native charts assigned (249 at 1.00.0, 59 at 1.01.0). Phoenix: 1,340 of 1,340 natives — 807 by video id, 471 by title, 65 from the wiki, 4 present at launch by elimination — plus 7 non-native charts in version playlists (Prime and Zero revivals, and the two La Cinquantaine charts of D9). Chart videos never name their version; the teaser title and the BGA descriptions do, and the new patch's playlist is the cleanest signal for the ongoing check-new-charts flow.
